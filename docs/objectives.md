# PolicyGPT: Government Policy & Public Scheme Intelligence Platform

## Project Objectives

The core objective of PolicyGPT is to build a full-stack web application using Angular and FastAPI that enables citizens, government officials, researchers, students, and organizations to access, search, analyze, and understand government policies, public welfare schemes, regulations, notifications, circulars, and official documents through a centralized digital platform.

### Key Goals
1. **Centralized Platform:** Provide a single source of truth for all government policies and schemes.
2. **Intelligent Discovery:** Offer intelligent search, filtering, and categorization mechanisms to help users easily discover relevant benefits.
3. **Eligibility Matching:** Implement an eligibility checker to help citizens determine which schemes they qualify for based on their personal profile.
4. **Enhanced Transparency:** Improve transparency and increase awareness of government initiatives among the general public.
5. **Stakeholder Collaboration:** Facilitate a unified ecosystem for various roles including citizens, government officials, researchers, and administrators to interact with policy data.
6. **Data-Driven Insights:** Provide analytics dashboards for government officials and administrators to track policy usage, citizen engagement, and overall impact.

## Target Audience
- Citizens
- Government Officials
- Researchers
- Students
- Non-Governmental Organizations (NGOs)
- Businesses
